About Toadette

Toadette is a character in Mario franchise. Her first appearance is in Mario Kart: Double Dash!! Toadette's Japanese name is トーデット or キノピコ (Kinopico).

Traits & Characteristics

  • Her bouncing pigtails
  • Her cheerful and energetic personality
  • Her occasional bursts of creativity

Hobbies & Interests

  • Playing sports like golf and tennis
  • Making music
  • Building things
