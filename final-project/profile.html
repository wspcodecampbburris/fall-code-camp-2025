Link

The Corageous Hero of Hyrule

About

A young and highly adventurous hero who makes it his mission to save the world. He bears the Triforce of Courage, which symbolises that he is chosen by the Goddess Hylia to seek justice and keep the world at peace. He is famous for wearing several different shades of green, his signature pointy hat, and elf-like ears with one blue ear piercing.

Traits & Characteristics

  • Kind
    • Link is the kind of guy to stop and help you get a cat out of a tree or return a lost object.
  • Brave
    • Having the Triforce of Courage, Link isn't afraid of monsters, spiders, or danger.
  • Strong
    • Link is able to wield a sword and carry pounds of equipment at all times, both up hills and underwater.
  • Clumsy
    • Despite being an epic hero, he is prone to tripping and falling asleep in class.
  • Dedicated
    • No matter how hard the task is, Link will find a way to get it done. This applies especially to things Zelda asks of him.

Hobbies & Interests

  • Wood Carving
    • Link displays several statues of animals, namely different birds, that he's carved around his room. He has gifted several to friends and family.
  • Cutting Grass
    • While it may seem like a chore to some, Link thoroughly enjoys cutting through grass with his sword. It is even his side business!
  • Catching Bugs
    • When wandering in nature, Link always has his bug net handy. His favorite bugs to catch include beetles, lightning bugs, and grasshoppers.
  • Saving Princesses
    • Link is always the one tasked with saving Princess Zelda, he even ranks the adventures based on the duration, how many new items he picks up, and how many places he got to see.
  • Racing Cars
    • Link is known as the best motorcycle rider in his group of friends. Mario always asks him for tips.
