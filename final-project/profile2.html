Marcassin

The Porcine Prince

About

Marcassin is the ruler of Hamelin, a major tech city, and one of the Great Sages. He is extremely talented and handsome, and looks up to his brother Gascon. His training includes hours of pouring over magical textbooks daily, all while dealing with Hamelin's affairs.

Traits & Characteristics

  • Brave
    • Marcassin will always fight to protect his kingdom and his friends.
  • Mature
    • Marcassin took over the throne at an early age, but was incredibly good at what he did.
  • Caring
    • Marcassin takes good care of all of his familiars, and feeds them treats on a regular basis to reward their good beavior.
  • Friendly
    • Marcassin is the kind of person who would stop to have a chat on the street, as long as he has the time.

Hobbies & Interests

  • Taking Care of Familiars
    • He loves training his familiars, pushing them to their limits, and rewarding them with rest and a hot meal afterwards. He has also been noted to let them sleep in cozy beds next to him.
  • Baking Pies and Cakes
    • Marcassin bakes treats for his party, and loves to experiment with different recipes. Sometimes this ends in disaster, but most times it ends in delicious chaos.
  • Practicing Magic
    • Marcassin pours through textbooks and ancient tomes. He is always preparing for the next battle.
  • Styling Fancy Outfits
    • He loves planning what he'll wear to his next public event, and his favorites include pig-inspired gear.
